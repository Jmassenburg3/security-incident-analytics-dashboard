# security-incident-analytics-dashboard

#Objective
--
Analyze data for unusual patterns and potential security concern.
Practice investigate and analytical thinking.
Document finding and communicate results clearly.

#Tools and Technologies
--
GitHub- project documentation and version control | Microsoft Excel-data organization, analysis and visualization

#Methodology
--
1. Organize and review the availible dataset.
2. Identify relevant fields, trends and unusual activity.
3. Recommend potential next steps based on the evidence.

#Key Findings
--
Finding 1: Phishing accounted for 30% of the dataset. This suggests phishing awareness and email security would be worthwhile areas to investigate.
Finding 2: Most incidents had high or critical severity. That is 70% of the incidents analyzed. A security team should prioritize incidents based on severity, potential impact, and urgency.
Finding 3: Most incidents were resolved at 80%.

#Skills Demonstrated
--
. Analytical and investigative reasoning.
. Data organization and interpretation.
. Identifying patterns and anomolies
. Technical documentation
. Security awareness
. Communicating findings and recommendations
